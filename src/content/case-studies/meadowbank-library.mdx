> **This is a demo case study.** Meadowbank Libraries is fictional, and so is every number on this page. Replace it with your own work.

## Overview

Meadowbank's library app had been installed by thousands of members, and opened by very few of them more than once.

## What we found

A week of interviews found one job people genuinely came back for: renewing a book before it went overdue. Everything else in the app was in the way of it.

## What changed

The home screen became a list of what you have borrowed and when each item is due, with renew one tap away. A reminder arrives three days before anything is due.

![The redesigned home screen, listing borrowed books with their due dates](/case-studies/meadowbank-library/cover.svg)

## Outcome

Monthly active members rose from 9% to 31% of installs in the first quarter after launch.
